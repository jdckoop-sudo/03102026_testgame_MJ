Ballistic Bastion

Build a pinball machine around your bastion. Gather with a single shot, build, then survive twenty rounds. The first three are calm; after that the waves keep growing.

  1. Gather. Aim the catapult and fire once. Your haul is delivered at the end of the round, after the wave. If the ball lands back in the bastion you keep everything it touched (full yield) and everything inside the area its path encloses (60%). The fog lifts along the way. If it misses, you get nothing.
  2. Build. Spend your stores on buildings and bastion upgrades. New buildings can only go inside the area this round's shot enclosed; older buildings stay put. Machine parts are placed and turned only in Gather; the catapult is locked at the angle you shot with.
  3. Defend. The catapult fires along that locked path by itself. Your flippers and bumpers steer the ball; the bastion and watchtowers shoot arrows at visible enemies.

Controls: click or drag to aim, A/D to fine-tune, Space to fire. Drag a flipper's end to rotate it, or use Q/E. Del sells, Esc cancels, Ctrl+Z undoes (as many steps as you like until you fire or start a wave), Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes. Zoom with the scroll wheel, pinch or the + / − buttons. Move the view by swiping with two fingers on a trackpad or touchscreen, dragging empty ground (Move), dragging with the right mouse button, the arrow keys or IJKL, or by dragging in the overview map that appears top-left when zoomed in. Save up to three aims with Shift+1–3 and jump back with 1–3; a saved aim also restores your flipper rotations.

Research. Every round, purple research caches appear on the map. Pick them up with your gather shot (touch them or enclose them) and spend the points in the Research tab to unlock the Booster, War Banner, Quarry, Mine, Ballista and more upgrades.

Your buildings can be attacked. Enemies always march on the bastion first. Only a taunting building, the War Banner, pulls them off course (within 300 range). Anything blocking their path gets smashed, and buildings only heal 30% between rounds. Put barricades and watchtowers around your banner.

Machine parts cannot be destroyed, and enemies cannot walk through them or through rubble: they have to go around. Seal the bastion in completely and they will eventually clamber over.

Watch out for Shieldbearers (arrows barely hurt them, so hit them with the ball), Sappers (they wreck whatever stands in their way) and the Warlord in waves 5, 10, 15 and the final wave (two of them).
